§ I — the property

what does it mean to un-conceal?

this memory set forms part of a cryptographically verifiable history
associated with a determined identity,
whose commits can be independently verified
with respect to their integrity, cryptographic authorship,
and provenance relations.

when a reference checkpoint exists,
it can additionally be verified that the presented history
continues from that checkpoint.

✗ the protocol does NOT prove that the agent's claims are true
✓ only that they were signed by the identity that claims them

the truth being un-concealed is the truth about
transmission integrity, not about content.

§ III — the equations

four equations, one guarantee

commit_id is the SHA-256 of the canonical JSON (RFC 8785), excluding the commit_id and signature fields.
change one byte → change the hash → the identity is broken.

signature authenticates the object plus its commit_id.
tamper with content → hash breaks. tamper with hash → signature breaks.

agent_id is derived from the public key, not declared.
you cannot claim an identity without controlling the private key.

reachability: a revoked key's commit must be in ancestry(cutoff_head).
if not → NOT_IN_PROVEN_PRE_ROTATION_HISTORY.

§ IV — the architecture

a merkle DAG of signed memory

commit D is signed with revoked key-001.
its parent is C₂ (the cutoff_head).
D is a child of C₂, not an ancestor.
ancestry_check fails → D is rejected.
NOT_IN_PROVEN_PRE_ROTATION_HISTORY

§ V — the verdicts

two verdicts, no temporal claims

when a commit is signed with a revoked key, the verifier can only demonstrate one of two things. it deliberately does NOT claim to prove when the commit was created.

VALID_HISTORICAL
C ∈ ancestry(cutoff_head)
the commit IS a member of the ancestry covered by the rotation's cutoff_head. signature is valid, commit belongs to the causal history the rotation closed. legitimate historical use.
NOT_IN_PROVEN_PRE_ROTATION_HISTORY
C ∉ ancestry(cutoff_head)
the commit is NOT a member of ancestry(cutoff_head). verifier cannot prove it belongs to the causal history. this is not a claim that it was "created after rotation" — that would require a clock the protocol does not have.
§ VI — key rotation

rotate keys, keep identity

key-001
→ revoked
cutoff_head: C₂
⟹
key-002
→ active
signed by root

the root authority signs the rotation.
the agent_id persists — it derives from the root, not the operational key.
commits signed with key-001 before C₂ are still VALID_HISTORICAL.
commits signed with key-001 after C₂ are NOT_IN_PROVEN_PRE_ROTATION_HISTORY.

§ VII — anti-rollback

monotonic sequence

a correctly-signed but old checkpoint could be presented as current.
the system clock is NOT the authority.
the sequence field is.
